Spanish Trainer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 24, 2026  ·  Developer: Dylan Mace

This Privacy Policy describes how Spanish Trainer (bundle ID: com.dylanmace.spanish-trainer) handles your information. Spanish Trainer is a personal vocabulary-learning app that uses spaced repetition to help you study Spanish with materials from your own tutor sessions.

1. Overview

Spanish Trainer is designed with privacy as a default. The app has no backend server of its own. All vocabulary data, study progress, and settings are stored locally on your device. The only outbound network requests are to third-party services you explicitly authorize, described below.

2. Information Collected and How It Is Used

Gmail (read-only access)

With your permission, Spanish Trainer connects to your Gmail account using Google OAuth with the gmail.readonly scope. This access is used solely to import vocabulary materials your language tutor has sent you. The app reads email content on-device; that content is never transmitted to any server operated by the developer.

Gmail data is read locally. It is not sold, shared, or uploaded to any developer-controlled server.

Vocabulary and study data

Words, definitions, example sentences, study history, and spaced-repetition scheduling data are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device except as described under Third-Party Services below.

Microphone

The app requests microphone access to support speech-recognition practice exercises. Audio captured during these exercises is processed on-device using the operating system's built-in speech framework. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by the app.

API credentials

Any API keys you enter (e.g., your Anthropic API key) are stored in the device's secure keychain via expo-secure-store and are never transmitted outside of the authorized API calls described below.

3. Third-Party Services

Spanish Trainer uses the following third-party services. Each service's own privacy policy governs how they handle data sent to them.

Google OAuth / Gmail API

Used to authenticate your Google account and read tutor emails. Google's use of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Spanish Trainer's Gmail access at any time via Google Account Permissions.

Anthropic Claude API

To classify and organize vocabulary content extracted from your tutor emails, the app sends text snippets to the Anthropic Claude API. Only the relevant text (vocabulary words and surrounding context) is sent — no personal identifiers, email metadata, or contact information are included in these requests. Anthropic's use of this data is governed by the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

No advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or data brokers are used in this app.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local SQLite database — vocabulary, definitions, and study progress stored on-device.
  • Secure keychain (expo-secure-store) — API keys and OAuth tokens stored in the iOS/Android secure enclave-backed keychain.
  • No developer-operated cloud storage — the developer does not operate any database, cloud storage bucket, or logging service that receives your data.

While the app implements reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately at the address below.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. The only data that leaves your device are the API calls described in Section 3, which are necessary to provide the app's core functionality and are made at your direction.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users or others, but given that no user data is stored on developer servers, such disclosure would be limited to information already on your own device.

6. Children's Privacy

Spanish Trainer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and deletion — All your study data lives on your device. You can delete the app at any time to remove all locally stored data.
  • Revoke Gmail access — Visit Google Account Permissions to revoke access at any time.
  • Microphone access — You can disable microphone access in your device's system settings at any time; this will disable speech-practice features.
  • API key removal — You can remove your Anthropic API key from within the app settings; this will disable AI classification features.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will provide a more prominent notice (such as an in-app notification). We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of Spanish Trainer after changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
